# SEO Authority Builder

## Overview

Authority is how Google and AI systems decide whether to trust and cite your content. Trust is the most important E-E-A-T pillar (Google's own words). Brand web mentions now correlate more strongly with AI visibility (0.664) than backlinks (0.218). Build entity authority, not just links.

> **Boundary:** company/brand entity authority → here; an individual professional's findability (personal site, GitHub, social, AI people-search) → `career-online-presence`.

## E-E-A-T Framework (2026)

E-E-A-T is **not a direct ranking factor** — it's a framework that shapes the algorithms. But the December 2025 core update significantly amplified these signals, and E-E-A-T now applies to **ALL content categories**, not just YMYL.

### Experience (Amplified Dec 2025)

Google detects experience through: personal pronouns indicating direct involvement, specific data points only from implementation, original photos with EXIF data, documented testing methodology, named models/versions.

**For e-commerce**: Staff-written descriptions showing product knowledge, real benchmark data, hands-on photos, comparison insights from actual use.

### Expertise

Deep understanding shown through structure, explanation, and contextualization — not just credentials. Cited sources, statistics (+22% AI visibility), quotations (+37% AI visibility).

### Authority

Brand mentions (linked AND unlinked), backlinks from reputable sources, direct brand searches. **Topical authority matters more than domain authority** for informational queries — small sites outrank high-DA with better topic coverage.

### Trust (Most Important)

User reviews, social proof, transparent sourcing. SSL, clear contact details, return policy (with MerchantReturnPolicy schema), privacy policy, cookie consent.

## Brand Entity Building

### Knowledge Graph

Google's Knowledge Graph: **54+ billion entities, 1.6+ trillion facts** (May 2024, post-cleanup removing 3B+ low-quality entities).

### Knowledge Panel Optimization

- **Not dependent on Wikipedia** — Google draws from 209,966+ trusted sources (Wikidata, Google Business Profile, Crunchbase, LinkedIn, social profiles, government databases)
- Create an **"Entity Home"** — one authoritative page (About page) that serves as Google's reference point
- Corroborate entity facts across **3+ independent authoritative sources**
- Timeline: 6-12 months for new brands, 2-4 months for established brands
- Claim your Knowledge Panel when it appears

### sameAs Schema

Link to ALL official profiles: LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, Trustpilot, Google Maps, Companies House, Wikidata. **sameAs URLs must be valid and accessible** — 404s hurt entity recognition.

## Social Signals

### Are They a Ranking Factor?

- **Google: No (direct), Yes (indirect).** Officially denied as direct factors. But 2024 leaked documents reveal NavBoost uses Chrome clickstream data and "siteAuthority" tracks brand authority — both influenced by social activity's downstream effects
- **Bing: Yes (direct).** Explicitly confirms social engagement as ranking factor
- **Local SEO (2026): Yes.** Whitespark's 2026 Local Ranking Factors report lists social signals as a NEW factor

### The Indirect Chain

Social activity → drives traffic → engagement signals (dwell time, CTR) → NavBoost picks this up. Additionally: Social presence → brand awareness → branded searches → brand authority signal.

### Platform Impact on SEO

| Platform | SEO Value | Key Action |
|----------|-----------|------------|
| **YouTube** | 0.737 AI visibility correlation (strongest single factor) | Create product showcases, build guides, benchmarks |
| **Reddit** | 1,328% visibility growth 2024; Google $60M/year deal | Genuine participation in relevant subreddits |
| **LinkedIn** | "Biggest winner of Social SEO in 2026" — treated as publishing platform | Publish industry content |
| **Google Business Profile** | Primary local ranking factor; 70% more location visits when complete | Complete every field, respond to reviews |

### Social Signal Services — Avoid

Buying likes, shares, or followers has **zero direct SEO impact**, is detectable, risks penalties, and wastes budget. Legitimate amplification = paid ads to real audiences, influencer partnerships, and genuinely shareable content.

## Reviews and Trust Signals

- **93% of buyers** check reviews before purchasing
- Trust badges: up to **42% more conversions** (CXL/Baymard)
- 80% of consumers don't trust businesses with inconsistent NAP
- Complete Google Business Profile: 2x more likely to be considered reputable
- Rich snippet star ratings: **+20-35% CTR**

### Review Strategy

- Aggregate reviews from third-party platforms (Trustpilot, Google Reviews) with AggregateRating schema
- Encourage verified purchase reviews with photos and specific details
- **Never fabricate or incentivize fake reviews** — Google cross-references signals
- Google Reviews velocity is the **3rd most important local ranking factor** (2026 Whitespark)

## Author Entity Building

Google now treats authors as entities separate from their sites. A recognized expert can lift rankings on any site they contribute to.

**Rules:**
- Every content page needs a real, verifiable author with byline linking to author page
- Author page must contain: professional photo, bio, qualifications, social profile links, evidence of experience
- **Never fabricate author credentials** — Google specifically targets fake E-E-A-T signals (January 2025 guidelines)
- Maintain consistent name across platforms for entity recognition

## Anti-Patterns

| Treat domain authority as key metric | Topical authority outranks DA for informational queries |
| Buy social signals (likes, shares) | Zero SEO impact, detectable, waste of money |
| Mass guest post for links | Dead strategy, penalized |
| Ignore unlinked brand mentions | 0.664 correlation with AI visibility — monitor and convert to links |
| Skip YouTube | 0.737 correlation — strongest single AI visibility factor |
