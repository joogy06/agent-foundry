# UK rates and thresholds — the single source for this skill family

<!-- REVIEW-BY: 2027-04-06 -->
**REVIEW-BY: 2027-04-06** · verified 2026-07-28 against the primary sources linked per figure.

**Every figure in the `accounting-uk-*` family comes from this file.** Nothing is inlined into prose
elsewhere, because a rate buried in a paragraph rots invisibly while still reading as authoritative.

## How to use this file

1. **Check `REVIEW_BY` before quoting anything.** Past it, the figures are *suspect until
   re-verified* — say so rather than using them silently.
2. **State the tax year with every figure.** "19%" is meaningless without "small profits rate, FY2026".
3. **Never apply a rate to a period it does not cover.** A straddling accounting period needs
   apportionment between financial years, not the later rate applied to the whole.
4. **When it matters, re-fetch the source.** The URLs are per-figure for exactly that reason.

> **Why this discipline exists.** While building this family, two of three secondary summaries
> checked were materially wrong: one gave the Corporation Tax main rate as 19% (that is the *small
> profits* rate — the main rate is 25%), another gave the employer secondary threshold as £50,270/yr
> when it is £5,000 — a 10× error. Both would have produced confidently incorrect tax figures that
> the user could not have spotted. Primary sources, always.

## Corporation Tax — financial years 2023 to 2026

FY starts 1 April. Source: [Corporation Tax rates and allowances](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/rates-and-allowances-corporation-tax/rates-and-allowances-corporation-tax) (page updated 1 April 2026).

| Item | Figure |
|---|---|
| Main rate — profits over £250,000 | **25%** |
| Small profits rate — profits under £50,000 | **19%** |
| Marginal relief lower limit | **£50,000** |
| Marginal relief upper limit | **£250,000** |
| Marginal relief standard fraction | **3/200** |

**Both limits are divided by the number of associated companies**, and pro-rated for accounting
periods shorter than 12 months. Source: [Marginal Relief for Corporation Tax](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/corporation-tax-marginal-relief).

**Deadlines** — different dates, separate penalties:
- **Pay:** 9 months + 1 day after the end of the accounting period
- **File CT600:** 12 months after the end of the accounting period

## VAT

Source: [Increasing the VAT registration threshold](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/vat-increasing-the-registration-and-deregistration-thresholds/increasing-the-vat-registration-threshold) · [Extension of MTD for VAT](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/extension-of-making-tax-digital-for-vat/extension-of-making-tax-digital-for-vat).

| Item | Figure |
|---|---|
| Registration threshold (12-month taxable turnover) | **£90,000** (from 1 Apr 2024) |
| Deregistration threshold | **£88,000** (from 1 Apr 2024) |
| Standard rate | **20%** |
| Reduced rate | **5%** |
| Zero rate | **0%** |
| Return + payment deadline | **1 month + 7 days** after period end |

**MTD for VAT applies to ALL VAT-registered businesses since April 2022**, regardless of turnover —
digital records plus an unbroken digital link through to submission.

## Payroll — tax year 2026/27

Source: [Rates and thresholds for employers 2026 to 2027](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2026-to-2027).

| Item | Weekly | Monthly | Annual |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Secondary threshold** (employer NI starts) | **£96** | **£417** | **£5,000** |
| **Primary threshold** (employee NI starts) | **£242** | **£1,048** | **£12,570** |

| Item | Figure |
|---|---|
| Employer secondary Class 1 NIC rate | **15%** |
| Employment Allowance | **£10,500** |
| National Living Wage, 21+ (from 1 Apr 2026) | **£12.71/hr** |

**Employment Allowance has specific restrictions for single-director companies** — see
[single-director companies and Employment Allowance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/employment-allowance-more-detailed-guidance/single-director-companies-and-employment-allowance-further-employer-guidance).
A misclaim here is common and recoverable by HMRC.

**RTI deadlines:** FPS **on or before every payday**; EPS where reclaims or a nil payment apply;
PAYE/NIC paid by the **22nd** electronically (the 19th is the *postal* deadline).

## Companies House

Source: [Filing after the joint service closed](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/closure-of-the-service-to-file-your-company-accounts-and-tax-return) · [Accounts changes from April 2028](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/companies-house-to-bring-in-changes-to-accounts-filing-from-april-2028).

| Item | Deadline / status |
|---|---|
| Annual accounts | 9 months after ARD (**21 months** from incorporation for a first period) |
| Confirmation statement | Annually, within **14 days of the review period end**; due even if nothing changed |
| Registered email address | Required since **4 March 2024** (not public) |
| Statement of lawful purpose | Required on every confirmation statement dated from **5 March 2024** |
| Identity verification (ECCTA) | Rollout began **18 November 2025**, transitioning |
| Joint HMRC/CH filing service | **Closed 31 March 2026** |
| Software-only accounts filing | **April 2028** (deferred from 2027, still under review) |

### Company size thresholds — periods beginning on or after 6 April 2025 (2 of 3)

| | Turnover | Balance sheet total | Employees |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Micro-entity** | ≤ £1m | ≤ £500,000 | ≤ 10 |
| **Small** | ≤ £15m | ≤ £7.5m | ≤ 50 |

Small-company **audit exemption** mirrors the small thresholds. Lost in an ineligible group or
regulated sector, or if members holding **10%+** demand an audit.

### Accounts filing changes — 1 April 2028 (ECCTA)

- Small and micro companies **must file a P&L**
- **Abridged accounts abolished**
- **Enhanced audit-exemption statement** naming which exemption is claimed

Source: [Changes to accounts](https://changestoukcompanylaw.campaign.gov.uk/changes-to-accounts/) ·
[Life of a company: accounts](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/life-of-a-company-annual-requirements/life-of-a-company-part-1-accounts)

## Known volatility — check these first at review

- **CT600 changes April 2027** — two new boxes for a 40% first-year allowance.
- **Companies House software-only filing, April 2028** — under review; the date has moved once.
- **MTD for Income Tax** phasing (April 2026 £50k → 2027 £30k → 2028 £20k) affects sole traders and
  landlords, **not** a Ltd's corporation tax, but a director may be caught personally.
- NLW, NIC thresholds and Employment Allowance are set annually at fiscal events.
