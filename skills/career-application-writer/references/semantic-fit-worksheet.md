# Semantic-Fit Worksheet

The evidence-ledger / coverage-matrix template for Stage 1 of `career-application-writer`, plus the honest-gap protocol. This is how the writer proves (to itself and the user) that every claim is backed and every gap is surfaced — never filled.

---

## Step 1 — Decompose the posting

From the target posting, extract four lists:
- **Scorecard criteria** — what they will actually evaluate against (often implied, not labelled).
- **Must-haves** — hard requirements.
- **Implied business problems** — *why* each requirement exists (the real need behind the line).
- **Knockout gates** — eligibility / work authorization / hard pass-fail items (answer these truthfully; they are distinct from content scoring).

## Step 2 — Build the coverage matrix

One row per criterion / must-have. Pull evidence ONLY from the verified achievement bank.

| Criterion (from posting) | Evidence (bank item) | Strength | Gap? | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| e.g. "automate controls so they're auditable" | Reconciliation platform — closed 2 audit findings | Strong | — | Lead bullet, top of experience |
| e.g. "model-risk experience" | Surveillance evidencing workstream | Medium | partial — no formal model-risk title | Frame honestly as evidencing work; note in rationale |
| e.g. "5 yrs Python" | 6 yrs across two roles | Strong | — | Surface in competencies + 2 bullets |
| e.g. "managed a team of 10+" | Led 4 engineers | — | **GAP** | Open question to user — do not inflate |

**Strength scale:** Strong (direct, quantified, recent) / Medium (adjacent or older) / — (none).

## Step 3 — Honest-Gap Protocol

For every row marked **GAP** or **partial**:
1. **Never** fabricate or inflate to close it. (HARD RULE 2.)
2. Decide the honest move:
   - *Real adjacent evidence exists* → frame it accurately (e.g., "led 4" stays "led 4"; do not write "led a large team").
   - *No evidence exists* → raise it as an open question to the user ("The posting wants 10+ direct reports; your bank shows 4 — is there team-lead scope we haven't captured?").
   - *Genuine disqualifier* → tell the user plainly; help them decide whether to apply, not how to disguise it.
3. Carry every unresolved gap into the Stage 5 "unresolved evidence questions" list.

## Step 4 — Order by fit

Rank covered criteria by (scorecard weight × evidence strength). That ranking drives bullet order in Stage 2 — not chronology-by-default.

---

## Output of this worksheet

- A completed coverage matrix (reusable for the next application to a similar role).
- A clean gap list (the honest open questions).
- An ordered evidence set feeding Stage 2 drafting.

> The worksheet is the integrity backbone of the pipeline: if a bullet isn't traceable to a matrix row, it doesn't go in the document.
