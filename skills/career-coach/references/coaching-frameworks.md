## Framework Quick Reference

### GROW (Parent intake)
- **G**oal: What do you want to achieve? Be specific and measurable.
- **R**eality: Where are you now? What have you tried? What's working?
- **O**ptions: What could you do? Generate breadth before depth.
- **W**ill: Which option will you act on? When? What might get in the way?

### STAR / STAR-L (Storytelling)
- **S**ituation: Context with specifics (scale, regulatory environment, team size)
- **T**ask: YOUR specific responsibility (not the team's)
- **A**ction: What YOU did, with decision rationale
- **R**esult: Quantified outcomes in metrics the audience cares about
- **L**earning: What you learned and applied subsequently (STAR-L extension)

### Personal SWOT (Assessment)
- **S**trengths (internal): Technical depth, domain expertise, org capital, track record
- **W**eaknesses (internal): Perception gaps, network gaps, experience gaps
- **O**pportunities (external): AI demand surge, regulatory changes, restructuring
- **T**hreats (external): Younger talent, offshoring, tech shifts, org politics

### SOAR (Assessment, forward-looking)
- **S**trengths: What do you do better than anyone else?
- **O**pportunities: What market trends favor you?
- **A**spirations: What do you want to be known for in 5 years?
- **R**esults: What measurable outcomes signal success?

### 70/20/10 (Planning)
- **70%** Experiential: stretch assignments, cross-functional projects, P&L ownership
- **20%** Social: mentoring, peer groups, executive coffee chats, structured feedback
- **10%** Formal: certifications, conferences, executive education, reading

### Power/Interest Grid (Leadership)
| | High Interest | Low Interest |
|---|---|---|
| **High Power** | Manage Closely (sponsors, decision-makers) | Keep Satisfied (CFO, board members) |
| **Low Power** | Keep Informed (end users, team members) | Monitor (peripheral teams) |

### BATNA (Transition/Negotiation)
- Best Alternative To a Negotiated Agreement
- Your BATNA = what you do if this offer/promotion doesn't meet expectations
- Higher BATNA = more negotiation leverage
- Always know your BATNA before entering any negotiation
