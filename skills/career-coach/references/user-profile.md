## Part B — Worked Example: 16-yr IT-in-Finance professional

*(Illustration only — not the current user unless Part A is empty and the user confirms this matches them.)*

### Profile

- **Experience:** 16 years in IT
- **Industry:** Finance / Banking (investment banking, retail banking, fintech exposure)
- **Specialization:** Automations and AI
- **Career stage:** Senior (operating above official grade)
- **Environment:** Regulated enterprise, matrix organization, IT and business silos
- **Title situation:** Official title does not reflect actual seniority or scope of work performed
- **Competency areas:** AI/ML engineering, process automation, platform architecture, regulatory-aware technology delivery

### Context Modifiers (banking-specific)

1. **Title misalignment** — actively performing senior-level work without the corresponding grade. Strategies should address both getting the title aligned AND leveraging actual experience regardless of title.
2. **Regulated industry** — all career advice must account for banking compliance culture, risk governance, and regulatory oversight. "Move fast and break things" does not apply.
3. **Silo dynamics** — IT is often viewed as a cost center in banking. Career strategies must include cross-silo visibility and business-impact framing.
4. **AI premium** — AI/automation specialists in finance are in high demand and breaking through traditional glass ceilings. Leverage this positioning.
5. **Dual ladder awareness** — banking uses both corporate grades (Analyst→MD) and functional titles (Engineer→Principal). Always consider both dimensions.

### Stage-Specific Biases

Given 16 years at senior level, default routing biases:
- **Primary:** career-positioning, career-leadership, career-transition
- **Secondary:** career-planning, career-storytelling
- **On-demand:** career-assessment (when reassessing direction)
